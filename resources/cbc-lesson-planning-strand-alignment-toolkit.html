What’s Inside the Toolkit

Engineered specifically for the Kenyan classroom context, this 5-page publication provides complete pedagogical workflows and practical templates.

1

Curriculum Alignment Matrix

Framework for mapping national goals through broad learning areas, strands, and sub-strands down to daily 40-minute lesson plans.

2

SLO Unpacking Tool

ABCD methodology (Audience, Behavior, Condition, Degree) to turn vague aspirations into observable, measurable student performance.

3

5-Phase Lesson Sequencing

Detailed time allocations and teacher/learner activity matrices for Hook, Exploration, Guided Practice, Independent Application, and Reflection.

4

Core Competency Evidence

Concrete observational triggers to document critical thinking, collaboration, communication, and digital literacy in every session.

5

Formative Assessment Rubric

4-level evaluation scale: Exceeding Expectations (4), Meeting Expectations (3), Approaching Expectations (2), Below Expectations (1).

6

Inclusion & Differentiated Support

Tiered multi-ability modifications (tiered tasks, peer pairings, multisensory aids) ensuring no learner is left behind.

7

Master Lesson Plan Template

Clean, standardized 10-field table ready for termly school inspection, teacher appraisal (TPAD), and daily classroom use.

8

Worked Classroom Model

Fully filled model for Grade 7 Integrated Science (Living Things: Simple Plant Classification) demonstrating all 5 phases in practice.

🇰🇪 Official Curriculum-Context Notice

This independently prepared professional resource supports the implementation of Kenya’s competency-based curriculum. Teachers should verify grade-specific strands, sub-strands and learning outcomes against the latest official KICD curriculum designs.
