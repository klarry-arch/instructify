What’s Covered in this Guide

Structured into 9 actionable modules designed to turn any classroom—regardless of power or connectivity constraints—into an active digital learning environment.

1

SAMR & TPACK Frameworks

Pedagogy-first technology adoption matrix to move teachers from simple substitution to transformative lesson redesign.

2

Visual Aid Design (6x6 Rule)

High-contrast, high-legibility presentation principles ensuring slides can be read clearly from the back of 60-learner Kenyan classrooms.

3

Smart Display Workflows

Interactive whiteboard routines, digital annotation tactics, touch calibration, and student spotlighting techniques.

4

Classroom Tablet Management

Established device routines: 'Lids at 45°', 'Screens face down', battery charging schedules, and safe classroom storage.

5

Child Online Protection

Safe educational search protocols, learner privacy safeguards, anti-cyberbullying rules, and computer lab security guidelines.

6

3-2-1 Cloud Backup Strategy

Safeguarding schemes of work and student marks: 3 copies of vital files across 2 media types with 1 secure offsite cloud backup.

7

Universal Design for Learning

Accessible digital learning strategies, text-to-speech tools, subtitle usage, and multi-modal sensory representations for all learners.

8

Low-Resource & Offline Solutions

Deploying Kiwix, Kolibri, and offline Open Educational Resources (OER) in off-grid or intermittent internet school settings.

9

8-Point Resource Vetting Rubric

A practical checklist for educators to evaluate external software, YouTube clips, and learning apps before classroom use.

🇰🇪 Official Curriculum-Context Notice

This independently prepared professional resource supports the implementation of Kenya’s competency-based curriculum. Teachers should verify grade-specific strands, sub-strands and learning outcomes against the latest official KICD curriculum designs.
