WHO WE ARE

Bridging Educational Theory with Real-World Classroom Impact

Instructify Kenya is a dedicated education consultancy, educator professional development, and digital learning organisation based in Nairobi, Kenya. We exist to solve the critical disconnect between high-level curriculum frameworks and the practical realities of everyday African classrooms.

From primary and secondary teachers implementing the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) to school administrators modernising their technology infrastructure, we tackle foundational challenges: rote instruction, low classroom digital integration, and resource limitations.

We partner with educators, private and public schools, corporate institutions, and development partners to deliver practical toolkits, hands-on training workshops, verifiable credentials, and strategic capacity-building roadmaps that produce measurable learning outcomes.
