WHO WE ARE

Connecting Knowledge, Creativity & Real-World Impact

Instructify Kenya is an education and training-focused organization dedicated to bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical classroom application. We work alongside teachers, school directors, institutional leaders, and education ministries to build resilient, learner-centered educational ecosystems.

We believe that true learning occurs when content is contextual, engaging, and directly applicable. By blending local African realities with global best practices, we help learning communities unlock their full potential.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Core Values

The eight principles that shape everything we build, teach, and stand for.

01

Curiosity

Celebrating questions, discovery, and the joy of lifelong learning.

Learning begins with meaningful questions. We encourage curiosity, investigation, reflection, and discovery so that learners can develop a deeper understanding of ideas.

02

Creativity

Designing imaginative, practical, and engaging learning experiences.

We use creative, learner-centred teaching approaches that combine effective educational practices with appropriate technology to make learning practical, engaging, and impactful.

03

Relevance

Rooting solutions in local cultures, materials, and community needs.

Learning solutions are designed around the realities, cultures, resources, and needs of the communities they serve, making education more relevant and meaningful.

04

Collaboration

Co-creating meaningful outcomes alongside educators and leaders.

Educators, learners, families, leaders, and communities work together towards common educational goals and meaningful, sustainable outcomes.

05

Clarity

Simplifying complex pedagogy and EdTech into intuitive, learner-friendly formats.

Complex ideas and digital tools are presented in clear, simple, and accessible ways, helping users build confidence and achieve mastery naturally.

06

Renewal

Believing every classroom milestone creates fresh opportunity for human growth.

Learning is a continuous journey. Every experience, challenge, and achievement provides an opportunity to gain knowledge, improve skills, and grow.

07

Integrity

Leading with honesty, humility, transparency, and uncompromised dedication.

Every decision and action is guided by honesty, responsibility, fairness, transparency, respect, and the protection of every learner’s dignity.

08

Excellence

Upholding world-class standards of educational quality across every service.

We are committed to exceptional quality, continuous improvement, and educational solutions that meet high professional and global standards.

OUR JOURNEY

Transforming Education Step by Step

From grassroots workshops to a nationwide LMS platform empowering thousands of Kenyan educators.

Phase 01 · Inception

Grassroots Training

Launched in-person bootcamps for primary and secondary teachers in Nairobi, focusing on foundational digital literacy and ICT classroom readiness.

Phase 02 · Alignment

CBC Curriculum Integration

Partnered with education stakeholders to pioneer practical lesson-planning toolkits and assessment rubrics for Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum.

Phase 03 · Expansion

Digital LMS Platform

Scaled our self-paced accredited courses online, providing teachers in remote and rural counties with on-demand certifications and verifiable digital badges.

Phase 04 · Innovation

Smart Classrooms & AI

Integrating smart boards, immersive VR learning tools, and AI pedagogy training to equip African learners for the future of work.
