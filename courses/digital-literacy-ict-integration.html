Digital Literacy & ICT Integration for Teachers

Section 01

Course Cover Page & Overview

Course Title: Professional Teacher Training Manual: Digital Literacy & ICT Integration for Kenyan Schools
Publisher: Instructify Kenya Education Technologies & Leadership Academy
Target Curriculum: Kenya KICD Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) & National Education Sector Strategic Plan (NESSP)
Accreditation Level: Teacher Professional Development (TPD) Certified CPD Module

This training manual provides a step-by-step, highly practical guide designed to build Kenyan teachers' confidence, digital competence, and pedagogical skill in integrating ICT into everyday classroom practice. Whether teaching in an urban school with a full computer lab or a rural school using a single smartphone and solar lantern, this manual empowers educators to create engaging, CBC-aligned learning experiences.

Section 02

Course Rationale

Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) explicitly mandates Digital Literacy as a core competency for every learner from Pre-Primary through Senior Secondary. However, effective digital learning cannot take place without digitally literate, skilled, and empowered teachers.

Key National Alignment:
  • KICD Digital Content Framework: Equipping teachers to navigate and utilize KICD Edu-Cloud resources.
  • Digital Literacy Programme (DLP): Maximizing the pedagogical impact of learner tablets, teacher laptops, and projectors.
  • 21st Century Skills: Shifting teacher role from knowledge transmission to learning facilitation through technology.

Section 04

Course Duration & Delivery Mode

Total Duration: 40 Professional Development Hours (Equivalent to 4 Continuing Professional Development Credits)

Blended Delivery Breakdown

  • Face-to-Face Workshops: 16 Hours (2-Day Intensive Hands-on Workshop)
  • School-Based Practical Implementation: 16 Hours (Lesson delivery, artifact creation)
  • Online LMS / Peer Collaboration: 8 Hours (Instructify Platform discussion & submission)

Low-Resource Adaptability

Designed for seamless delivery in low-connectivity areas using offline mobile LMS downloads, SMS/WhatsApp micro-learning snippets, and printable offline worksheets.

Section 05

General Learning Outcomes

By the end of this professional course, Kenyan teachers will be able to:

  1. Demonstrate proficient use of everyday digital tools, operating systems, cloud storage, and mobile devices for professional educational work.
  2. Apply the TPACK and SAMR frameworks to design CBC-aligned lesson plans that meaningfully integrate technology.
  3. Produce professional digital learning resources including CBC worksheets, slide presentations, audio micro-lessons, and automated assessment marksheets.
  4. Utilize digital assessment tools (Google Forms, Kahoot, Quizizz) to track learner progress and generate instant formative feedback.
  5. Promote digital citizenship, online safety, copyright compliance, and cyber-hygiene in their schools and communities.
  6. Implement inclusive, low-resource ICT strategies suitable for rural, remote, or large-class Kenyan environments.
  7. Construct a professional Teacher E-Portfolio showcasing verified digital classroom outputs and a 1-year School ICT Action Plan.
Section 06

Section 08

Detailed Training Modules (1 to 10)

Module 1: Foundations of Digital Literacy & Cloud Storage
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Participants will navigate operating systems, organize folders effectively, manage cloud storage (Google Drive / OneDrive), and share documents securely.

Detailed Content Notes: Hardware basics, file extensions (.pdf, .docx, .xlsx, .jpg, .mp3), folder directory structuring by Grade & Learning Area, Google Drive file sharing permissions (Viewer vs Editor), offline file sync.

Kenyan Classroom Example: A Grade 5 Science teacher creates a shared Google Drive folder named Grade5_Science_CBC_2026 and organizes subfolders for Lesson Plans, Assessment Rubrics, and Learner Photos.

Practical Activity: Create a structured cloud storage folder hierarchy and share a sample document with a fellow teacher via email link with "Viewer" rights.

Assessment Task: Submit the URL link to your shared subject Google Drive folder.

Required Resources: Laptop/smartphone, internet connection, Gmail account.

Low-Resource Alternative: Using a OTG Flash Drive to organize folders on a smartphone or school office computer offline.

Participant Output: Shared Cloud Folder Hub with subject subfolders.

Reflection Question: How does structured file management reduce administrative preparation time before a lesson?

Module 2: Pedagogical Frameworks for ICT Integration (TPACK & SAMR)
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Evaluate technology integration using the SAMR model (Substitution, Augmentation, Modification, Redefinition) and TPACK (Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge).

Detailed Content Notes: Moving beyond using technology as a mere projector (Substitution) to learner-centered creation and global collaboration (Redefinition). Aligning pedagogy with content and appropriate digital tools.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Instead of drawing the human heart on a chalkboard (Substitution), a Social Studies/Science teacher uses a 3D interactive heart animation app or digital video simulation (Modification/Redefinition).

Practical Activity: Take an existing traditional paper lesson and redesign it across all 4 SAMR levels.

Assessment Task: Fill out the SAMR Lesson Redesign Worksheet.

Required Resources: Sample printed CBC lesson designs, SAMR reference chart.

Low-Resource Alternative: Facilitated group discussion using printed SAMR case study handouts.

Participant Output: SAMR Lesson Transformation Concept Note.

Reflection Question: Why is technology without sound pedagogy ineffective in achieving CBC learning outcomes?

Module 3: Word Processing & CBC Worksheet Formatting
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Design, format, and print professional CBC learner worksheets, assessment rubrics, and activity sheets using MS Word or Google Docs.

Detailed Content Notes: Typography, table formatting, incorporating images, bullet points, headers/footers, generating PDFs for printing, saving as templates.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Designing a Grade 4 Mathematics formative assessment activity sheet with clear tables, diagrams of geometric shapes, and a CBC self-assessment rubric at the bottom.

Practical Activity: Format a 2-page CBC activity worksheet complete with KICD learning area headers, images, and assessment rating scales (Exceeding Expectations, Meeting Expectations, Approaching, Below).

Assessment Task: Submit the PDF version of your customized subject worksheet.

Required Resources: MS Word / Google Docs, sample educational clipart/images.

Low-Resource Alternative: Mobile Google Docs app formatting on a mobile phone.

Participant Output: Print-ready CBC Learner Activity Worksheet.

Reflection Question: How does clear document formatting improve readability for young CBC learners?

Module 4: Spreadsheets for Assessment & Formative Tracking
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Utilize MS Excel / Google Sheets to record marks, calculate averages, sort student data, build CBC performance rubrics, and generate visual charts.

Detailed Content Notes: Data entry, basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNTIF), conditional formatting for CBC performance bands (EE, ME, AE, BE), generating column charts, freezing panes, filtering lists.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Creating a Grade 7 Junior Secondary Class Marksheet that automatically highlights learners scoring "Below Expectations" in red for targeted remedial support.

Practical Activity: Build a 30-student class marksheet with 5 learning area columns, auto-calculated total/average, and conditional color bands.

Assessment Task: Submit the complete Excel/Sheets file with working formulas.

Required Resources: MS Excel or Google Sheets software.

Low-Resource Alternative: Using WPS Office app on a mobile phone to manage student marks.

Participant Output: Automated CBC Class Marksheet & Performance Tracker.

Reflection Question: How does visual data analytics assist in early identification of learning gaps?

Module 5: Digital Presentations & Visual Content Creation
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Create visual, engaging, non-cluttered slide presentations (PowerPoint / Google Slides / Canva) to support classroom instruction.

Detailed Content Notes: Slide layout rules (6x6 rule), inserting diagrams & video links, slide transitions, animation timing, presenter view, exporting slides as PDF handouts.

Kenyan Classroom Example: A History / Social Studies teacher designs a 10-slide visual deck on "Physical Features of East Africa" featuring high-resolution maps, local landscape photos, and discussion prompts.

Practical Activity: Build a 10-slide presentation for a selected CBC topic incorporating visual media and an interactive reflection slide.

Assessment Task: Deliver a 5-minute micro-teaching presentation using your slide deck.

Required Resources: Presentation software, projector or laptop screen.

Low-Resource Alternative: Saving slide decks as images/PDFs to share on mobile phones via Bluetooth or WhatsApp.

Participant Output: 10-Slide Subject Presentation Deck.

Reflection Question: Why is visual content essential for maintaining learner engagement in CBC classrooms?

Module 6: Digital Communication & Online Classrooms
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Set up and manage digital learning channels including Google Classroom, professional teacher WhatsApp groups, and video conferencing tools (Zoom / Teams).

Detailed Content Notes: Creating virtual classes, posting announcements & assignments, email etiquette for educators, WhatsApp broadcast lists for parents, managing online student participation safely.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Creating a Grade 8 Agriculture WhatsApp Study Group with established posting guidelines for homework submissions and parent announcements.

Practical Activity: Create a demo Google Classroom or WhatsApp Subject Channel, invite 3 peer teachers, and post an assignment with a due date.

Assessment Task: Submit screenshots of your configured online classroom environment.

Required Resources: Google Account, Smartphone with WhatsApp, Zoom/Teams installed.

Low-Resource Alternative: WhatsApp Community/Broadcast Group set up on a basic smartphone.

Participant Output: Functional Digital Classroom / Communication Channel.

Reflection Question: What boundaries must teachers maintain when communicating with parents and learners online?

Module 7: CBC-Aligned ICT Lesson Planning & Resource Creation
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Integrate digital tools, video clips, audio recordings, and interactive software directly into standard KICD lesson plan formats.

Detailed Content Notes: Mapping ICT tools to specific CBC Learning Outcomes, Strand & Sub-strand alignment, creating short audio explanations (micro-lessons) using smartphone voice recorders, sourcing copyright-free educational media.

Kenyan Classroom Example: A Kiswahili teacher records a 3-minute audio story (Insha/Hadithi) on a smartphone for pupils to listen to and analyze during a listening comprehension lesson.

Practical Activity: Write an ICT-infused CBC Lesson Plan and record a 2-minute audio micro-lesson or instructional voice note.

Assessment Task: Submit the completed Lesson Plan and Audio File.

Required Resources: Lesson Plan Template, Smartphone Voice Recorder.

Low-Resource Alternative: Utilizing pre-stored audio clips on a basic feature phone memory card.

Participant Output: Integrated ICT CBC Lesson Plan & Audio Micro-Lesson.

Reflection Question: How does audio-visual media support learners with different learning styles and abilities?

Module 8: Digital Quizzes & Formative Assessment Tools
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Construct self-grading digital quizzes and interactive review games using Google Forms, Kahoot, or Quizizz to evaluate learner understanding in real time.

Detailed Content Notes: Multiple choice & short answer questions, setting auto-grade keys, adding feedback explanations for incorrect choices, instant data analytics, Gamified learning with Kahoot/Quizizz.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Creating a 10-question Google Forms self-grading quiz for Grade 6 CRE/IRE that provides instant feedback to pupils upon submission.

Practical Activity: Design a 10-question self-grading digital quiz with automated scoring and feedback hints.

Assessment Task: Share the live quiz link and submit the auto-generated response summary sheet.

Required Resources: Google Forms / Kahoot / Quizizz platform.

Low-Resource Alternative: Paper-based Plickers cards or offline SMS-based quick quizzes.

Participant Output: 10-Question Self-Grading Digital Quiz.

Reflection Question: How does automated grading free up teacher time for targeted remedial instruction?

Module 9: Digital Citizenship, Online Safety, Copyright & Inclusion
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Teach digital citizenship, cyber-hygiene, protection against online risks, open educational resources (Creative Commons), and inclusive ICT adaptations for learners with special needs.

Detailed Content Notes: Safe password practices, cyberbullying prevention, digital footprint, Creative Commons licensing, screen reader accessibility, text-to-speech tools for inclusive education.

Kenyan Classroom Example: Drafting a poster on "5 Rules for Staying Safe Online" displayed in the school computer laboratory or classroom wall.

Practical Activity: Design an Online Safety Poster/Infographic and draft an Inclusive ICT Accommodation Strategy for learners with physical or visual impairments.

Assessment Task: Submit the E-Safety Poster and Inclusion Plan.

Required Resources: Canva / Word / Poster paper.

Low-Resource Alternative: Hand-drawn safety rules poster created during group workshops.

Participant Output: School E-Safety Poster & Inclusive ICT Strategy Guide.

Reflection Question: What role do teachers play in safeguarding learners from internet dangers in Kenya today?

Module 10: Teacher E-Portfolios & School ICT Action Planning
4 Hours (1 Credit)

Learning Outcomes: Assemble a comprehensive digital portfolio of all classroom outputs and draft a realistic 1-year School ICT Action Plan for equipment maintenance, staff capacity building, and integration.

Detailed Content Notes: E-Portfolio curation (Google Sites / PDF Portfolio), reflecting on digital growth, conducting school ICT SWOT analysis, budgeting for internet/power, setting up ICT peer mentorship in school.

Kenyan Classroom Example: A lead teacher compiles their lesson plans, marksheets, quizzes, and slide decks into a single Google Site E-Portfolio to showcase to the TSC Quality Assurance Officer during appraisal.

Practical Activity: Assemble the complete Teacher E-Portfolio containing all 8 module outputs and write a 2-page School ICT Action Plan.

Assessment Task: Submit the published E-Portfolio URL link for final grading.

Required Resources: Google Sites / PDF Compiler, Instructify E-Portfolio Rubric.

Low-Resource Alternative: Organizing all digital outputs into a master ZIP archive or organized Flash Drive folder portfolio.

Participant Output: Published Teacher Digital E-Portfolio & 1-Year School ICT Action Plan.

Reflection Question: How will you sustain and mentor other teachers in your school after completing this course?

Section 10

Assessment Rubrics, Final Portfolio & Certification

Master Portfolio Submission Checklist

To qualify for the Instructify Kenya Certificate of Completion in Digital Literacy & ICT Integration, participants must compile and submit the following 8 classroom outputs in their Digital E-Portfolio:

  1. Artifact 1: Shared Subject Cloud Folder Link (Google Drive / OneDrive)
  2. Artifact 2: SAMR Lesson Redesign Concept Note
  3. Artifact 3: Print-Ready Formative CBC Learner Worksheet (PDF)
  4. Artifact 4: Automated CBC Class Marksheet with Working Formulas (.xlsx)
  5. Artifact 5: 10-Slide Interactive Lesson Presentation Deck (.pptx / Link)
  6. Artifact 6: Integrated ICT CBC Lesson Plan & Audio Micro-lesson File (.mp3)
  7. Artifact 7: Live 10-Question Self-Grading Digital Quiz Link (Google Forms / Quizizz)
  8. Artifact 8: School E-Safety Poster & 1-Year School ICT Action Plan

Certification Assessment Rubric

Criteria Exceeding Expectations (4) Meeting Expectations (3) Approaching Expectations (2)
Pedagogical Alignment (TPACK/SAMR) Technology redefines learning activities; seamless CBC integration. Technology modifies or augments lesson activities effectively. Technology is used as simple substitution without added value.
Technical Execution Flawless formatting, working formulas, error-free digital quizzes. Minor technical errors that do not hinder educational utility. Multiple broken links, missing formulas, or unformatted text.
Contextual Relevancy Highly tailored for Kenyan CBC context with low-resource workarounds. Relevant to Kenyan school environment with basic adaptations. Generic content lacking alignment with local curriculum needs.
Certificate Completion Requirements:
  • Attendance & active participation in 80%+ of workshop/online sessions.
  • Successful submission of all 8 E-Portfolio Classroom Artifacts.
  • Attainment of a minimum score of 70% on the final portfolio assessment rubric.
