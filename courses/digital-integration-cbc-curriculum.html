📘 Section 1 • Pedagogical & Digital Foundations

Foundations for Educators & Learners

In Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) and Competency-Based Education (CBE), technology is not treated as an isolated computer-lab subject. Instead, it serves as an inquiry accelerator and cognitive scaffold integrated across all subjects. The educator transitions from a dispenser of memorized notes into a facilitator who orchestrates collaborative discovery, problem-solving, and digital creation.

🎓 EDUCATOR PEDAGOGICAL FOUNDATIONS Curriculum Delivery & Assessment Standards

🎯 The 7 Core Competencies Infusion

Digital integration directly fosters all 7 KICD Core Competencies: Communication & Collaboration (co-authoring Word/PowerPoint projects), Critical Thinking & Problem Solving (formulating Excel equations), Creativity & Imagination (digital storytelling and interactive multimedia), Citizenship (civic presentations), Learning to Learn (navigating self-paced slide decks), Self-Efficacy (digital portfolio mastery), and Digital Literacy (ethical software operation).

📈 The SAMR Model in Kenyan Classrooms

Substitution: Typing a handwritten essay into Word.
Augmentation: Adding spelling tools and hyperlinked research citations.
Modification: Using Excel to model variables and graph experimental predictions.
Redefinition: Designing a branching, multimedia presentation in PowerPoint with audio narration shared with peer schools nationwide.

🧠 The TPACK Framework

Effective digital integration occurs at the sweet spot of Technological Knowledge (TK), Pedagogical Knowledge (PK), and Content Knowledge (CK). A teacher does not just teach "how to use Excel"; they use Excel to deepen a learner's conceptual understanding of Mathematical probability or Agricultural yield calculations.

📖 Section 3 • Authoritative Glossary & Shortcuts

Definition of Terms & Student Hotkeys

To teach, evaluate, and learn accurately, both educators and students must share a precise, standardized understanding of curriculum terms, software architecture, and speed workflows:

🎓 EDUCATOR & CBC CURRICULUM GLOSSARY Standardized KICD Framework Terminology

🇰🇪 Kenyan CBC / CBE Curriculum Terms

Strand & Sub-strand

Strand: A broad thematic focus area in curriculum design (e.g., Numbers in Mathematics, Living Things in Science).
Sub-strand: A specific sub-topic or modular unit within a strand (e.g., Fractions or The Human Skeleton).

Specific Learning Outcomes (SLOs)

Observable, measurable statements describing the knowledge, skills, and values a learner must demonstrate by the end of a lesson, written using action verbs.

Pertinent and Contemporary Issues (PCIs)

Cross-cutting societal issues integrated into lessons, including environmental awareness, financial literacy, peace education, health, and child rights.

Formative vs. Summative Assessment

Formative (Assessment FOR Learning): Ongoing diagnostic checks and worksheets during lessons to adjust teaching.
Summative (Assessment OF Learning): Evaluation at the end of a term or level to gauge standard attainment.

4-Level Competency Descriptors

• Exceeding Expectations (EE): Surpasses requirements with autonomy and depth.
• Meeting Expectations (ME): Consistently satisfies all outcome criteria.
• Approaching Expectations (AE): Demonstrates fundamental criteria with occasional help.
• Below Expectations (BE): Requires intensive, guided remediation.

Digital Learning Artefact & Portfolio

Artefact: Any tangible digital product created by the learner (e.g., spreadsheet model, Word report, presentation).
Portfolio: A structured repository documenting learner progress and competency growth over time.

💻 Productivity Applications Architecture

Microsoft Word: Document Hierarchy & Styles

Structuring text using Heading 1, Heading 2, and Body text styles to ensure readability, accessible screen reading, and automatic Table of Contents generation.

Microsoft Word: Section Breaks & Tables

Dividing a document to allow independent page orientations (e.g., landscape rubric inside portrait lesson plan) and structured data cell formatting.

Microsoft Excel: Relative vs. Absolute Cell Referencing

Relative (A1): Shifts coordinates when copied.
Absolute ($A$1): Locks row and column coordinates, essential when calculating weighted percentages or referencing constant values.

Microsoft Excel: Conditional Formatting & Formulas

Formula: A calculation expression (e.g., =SUM(B2:B20), =AVERAGE(C2:G2)).
Conditional Formatting: Automated styling rules that color cells dynamically based on criteria (e.g., Green for EE, Red for BE).

Microsoft PowerPoint: Slide Master

The master design blueprint controlling fonts, logos, backgrounds, and universal layout placeholders across all slides in a presentation.

Microsoft PowerPoint: Trigger Animations & Non-Linear Navigation

Trigger Animation: An effect that only plays when a learner clicks a specific designated shape/button (enables gamified quizzes).
Non-Linear Navigation: Using action buttons to jump to specific slides based on user choice rather than chronological order.

📝 Section 4 • Microsoft Word Practical

Microsoft Word in Action: Lesson Planning, Reports & Rubrics

In this section, educators learn to design standardized lesson plans and descriptive rubrics, while students master typesetting high-scoring academic project reports with automated tables of contents and citations:

🎓 EDUCATOR TRACK: LESSON PLANS & FORMATIVE RUBRICS Curriculum Preparation & Assessment Design
📄 Practical Activity 4.1: Authoring a Standard 40-Minute CBC Lesson Plan

Follow these practical steps to design a standardized CBC lesson plan template in Microsoft Word:

  1. Page Configuration: Set Margins to Normal (1 inch / 2.54 cm). Apply Heading 1 (18pt Bold, Royal Blue) for the Institution and Subject Name.
  2. Administrative Header Grid: Insert a 4-column × 4-row table with light grey borders containing: Grade, Learning Area, Strand, Sub-strand, Date, and Duration (40 Minutes).
  3. Specific Learning Outcomes (SLOs): Write 3 distinct outcomes (Knowledge, Skills, Values) using observable verbs: "By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to..."
  4. Core Competency & PCI Callout Box: Insert a 1-cell shaded table with an icon to map targeted core competencies and contemporary issues.
  5. 3-Stage Instructional Sequence Table: Insert a 3-column table (Time | Facilitator Activities | Learner Activities & Digital Tool Usage):
    • Introduction (5-8 mins): Provocation or essential driving question.
    • Lesson Development (25 mins): Collaborative inquiry, hands-on tasks, peer discussion.
    • Conclusion & Reflection (7 mins): Plenary summary, formative self-assessment.
  6. Differentiated Support Section: Add a dedicated sub-section specifying accommodations for learners needing extra scaffolding or extension tasks.
📊 Practical Activity 4.2: Constructing 4-Level Competency Rubrics in Word Tables

CBC assessment requires qualitative descriptors across 4 levels. Here is the architectural blueprint to construct in Word:

Assessment Criteria Exceeding Expectations (4) Meeting Expectations (3) Approaching Expectations (2) Below Expectations (1)
Inquiry Execution & Investigation Autonomously formulates original hypotheses, executes steps flawlessly, and evaluates experimental anomalies with high depth. Accurately conducts all required steps of the investigation and records valid empirical observations. Follows basic steps with occasional guidance; observations lack full consistency. Unable to follow inquiry protocol without continuous one-on-one intervention.
Application of Digital Tools Creatively selects advanced formatting, styles, and tools to present data in an original, engaging format. Correctly uses all required digital tools to format and present findings cleanly. Uses basic software features with minor formatting errors or omissions. Struggles to navigate software; requires frequent technical assistance.

🎒 STUDENT TRACK: ACADEMIC PROJECT REPORTS & FORMATTING Hands-On Typesetting & Self-Check
📝 Practical Activity 4.3 (For Students): Formatting an Academic Project Report in Word

Learn how to turn a plain text document into a publication-grade school project or lab investigation report:

  1. Title Page & Layout: Set Page Size to A4 with 1-inch margins. Type a bold project title (24pt), subtitle, your name, grade, school name, and date. Insert a Page Break (Ctrl+Enter).
  2. Heading Hierarchy: Apply Heading 1 (16pt Bold) to main chapters (e.g., 1. Introduction, 2. Methodology, 3. Findings, 4. Conclusion) and Heading 2 (13pt SemiBold) to sub-topics.
  3. Automated Table of Contents: On Page 2, click References → Table of Contents → Automatic Table 1. Notice Word builds page numbers automatically!
  4. Structured Data Tables & Captions: Click Insert → Table (4×5). Shade the top header row in dark slate. Right-click the table → select Insert Caption → Table 1: Field Investigation Data.
  5. Citations & Footnotes: At the end of a cited fact, click References → Insert Footnote to credit your textbook or educational source.

📊 Section 5 • Microsoft Excel Practical

Microsoft Excel in Action: Trackers, Modeling & Charting

Spreadsheets provide computational power for educators to automate continuous assessment grading, and empower students to build financial models and plot scientific data:

🎓 EDUCATOR TRACK: AUTOMATED CBC GRADEBOOKS Continuous Assessment & KICD Reporting
📑 Practical Activity 5.1: Building an Automated CBC Formative Assessment Tracker

Build an automated continuous assessment tracker that converts numeric marks into KICD qualitative descriptors (EE, ME, AE, BE) and color-codes results:

1. Calculating Student Averages:
=AVERAGE(D5:H5)

Calculates the average score across 5 formative tasks for learner in row 5.

2. Nested IF Function for KICD Competency Level:
=IF(I5>=3.5, "EE", IF(I5>=2.5, "ME", IF(I5>=1.5, "AE", "BE")))

Automatically assigns performance ratings: EE (3.5+), ME (2.5 - 3.4), AE (1.5 - 2.4), and BE (below 1.5).

3. Class Performance Distribution (COUNTIF):
=COUNTIF($J$5:$J$44, "EE")

Counts how many students in the 40-learner cohort attained "EE" for reporting.

4. Conditional Formatting Rules:
  • Highlight Cell Rules → Equal To "EE" → Light Green Fill with Dark Green Text.
  • Highlight Cell Rules → Equal To "ME" → Light Blue Fill with Dark Blue Text.
  • Highlight Cell Rules → Equal To "AE" → Light Yellow Fill with Dark Yellow Text.
  • Highlight Cell Rules → Equal To "BE" → Light Red Fill with Dark Red Text (Immediate visual indicator for remediation).

🎯 Educator Practical Excel Deliverable

Build and submit a 40-student CBC Formative Assessment spreadsheet (.xlsx) with at least 5 assessment columns. Include automated calculation formulas (AVERAGE, IF, COUNTIF), 4-color conditional formatting for competency levels, and an embedded summary chart showing classroom competency distribution.

🎒 STUDENT TRACK: BUDGETING & SCIENCE DATA MODELING Hands-On Calculations & Chart Generation
📊 Practical Activity 5.3 (For Students): Building a Student Budget & Science Growth Model

Follow these practical steps to build a 2-sheet real-world workbook in Microsoft Excel:

Sheet 1: Weekly Student Budget Planner
  1. In cell A1, title the sheet: "My Weekly School Term Budget". Format in 16pt Bold with light green shading.
  2. In cells A4:B7, list your income sources (e.g. Pocket money, Chores, Academic award). Sum total income using:
    =SUM(B4:B7)
  3. In cells D4:E10, list your anticipated expenses (e.g. Exercise books, Transport, Snacks, Art supplies). Sum total expenses using:
    =SUM(E4:E10)
  4. In cell E12, compute your net savings surplus:
    =B8 - E11
  5. Select your expense items → click Insert → Pie Chart / 2-D Donut to see where your money goes!
Sheet 2: Biology Seedling Growth Rate Tracker
  1. Create a table with columns: Day (1-14) | Plant A - Sunlight (cm) | Plant B - Shade (cm).
  2. Enter empirical daily heights. Compute the Average Growth using:
    =AVERAGE(B4:B17)
  3. Find the maximum height reached using:
    =MAX(B4:B17)
  4. Highlight cells A3:C17 → click Insert → Line Chart with Markers. Add chart title, axis labels, and a visible legend.

🎯 Student Practical Excel Deliverable

📽️ Section 6 • Microsoft PowerPoint Practical

Microsoft PowerPoint in Action: Lessons, Games & Pitch Decks

Move beyond passive slide shows. Educators discover how to transform slides into interactive inquiry games, while students learn to build compelling, multimedia school project pitch decks:

🎓 EDUCATOR TRACK: BRANCHING LESSONS & FORMATIVE GAMES Interactive Inquiry & Animation Triggers
🗺️ Practical Activity 6.1: Authoring Non-Linear Branching Presentations

Non-linear slide decks empower learners to explore content at their own pace based on interest and inquiry:

  1. Master Navigation Template: In View → Slide Master, create persistent buttons for "Home", "Strand Index", and "Glossary" so navigation remains uniform across all slides.
  2. Interactive Home Dashboard Slide: Insert visual icon tiles representing Sub-strands (e.g. In Geography: Rift Valley Lakes | Highlands | Coastal Plains).
  3. Hyperlink Binding: Right-click each shape → select Hyperlink / Action → Place in This Document → link to the corresponding content slide.
  4. Branching Remediation & Challenge: Create an exploration question slide. If a student chooses an incorrect answer, hyperlink them to an explanation review slide; if correct, branch to an advanced extension challenge!
🎮 Practical Activity 6.2: Designing Gamified Trigger-Based Quiz Decks

Transform formative assessment checks into engaging classroom games using PowerPoint Animation Triggers:

  1. Create a question slide with 4 clickable answer option shapes (Option A, B, C, D).
  2. Insert two feedback text banners: a green banner stating "Correct! Excellent CBC reasoning" and a red banner stating "Try Again! Review the concept".
  3. Open the Animation Pane. Apply an Entrance Animation (e.g., Zoom or Fade) to the green banner.
  4. Select the animation → click Timing → Trigger → Start effect on click of... → select the correct answer button!
  5. Repeat trigger configuration for the wrong answer buttons to display the remedial banner.
  6. Test in Slide Show mode: learners receive instant interactive feedback when tapping answers on an interactive board or classroom computer.

🎯 Educator Practical PowerPoint Deliverable

Author and submit an interactive 10-slide PowerPoint presentation (.pptx) aligned with a specific CBC sub-strand. The deck must include a Slide Master navigation menu, non-linear hyperlinked action buttons, multimedia visual assets, and at least one trigger-based gamified quiz slide.

🎒 STUDENT TRACK: INTERACTIVE SCHOOL PROJECT PITCH DECKS Storytelling, Media Embedding & Presentation Skills
📽️ Practical Activity 6.3 (For Students): Designing an Interactive Project Pitch Deck

Follow these practical steps to design a captivating 8-to-10 slide presentation for your school science fair or social studies project:

  1. Widescreen Theme & Title: Set Slide Size to Widescreen (16:9). Choose a crisp, high-contrast theme (dark blue or deep forest green background with white text). Include project title, team members, and a striking high-resolution hero photo.
  2. Interactive Agenda Slide: Build an interactive table-of-contents slide using 4 rounded rectangle tiles (1. The Challenge, 2. Field Data, 3. Our Solution, 4. Action Steps). Hyperlink each button directly to its target slide.
  3. Embedding Live Data Charts: Copy your comparative growth chart from Excel → Paste into Slide 4 choosing Keep Source Formatting & Link Data. Notice how updates in Excel refresh in your deck!
  4. Adding Voiceover Narration: Click Insert → Audio → Record Audio. Record a 30-second summary introducing your solution to simulate a live conference pitch.
  5. Interactive Audience Question: Create an end-of-deck Q&A slide with clickable multiple-choice buttons that flash green when your classmates tap the right answer.
✅ Student PowerPoint Self-Check Checklist:

🎯 Student Practical PowerPoint Deliverable
