Accessibility Statement & Accommodations

Instructify Kenya is dedicated to ensuring that digital education, training materials, and learning management systems are accessible and barrier-free for learners and educators of all abilities.

Request an Accommodation Report an Accessibility Barrier

Our Accessibility Commitment

At Instructify Kenya, we believe that education must be universally accessible. In accordance with the Constitution of Kenya (Article 54), the Persons with Disabilities Act, and international accessibility standards, our platform adheres to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

We continually test and update our web pages, LMS interfaces, course player, and downloadable documents to remove digital barriers and provide equal access for people with visual, hearing, motor, cognitive, and learning disabilities.

Implemented Accessibility Features

⌨️

Full Keyboard Navigation

Every button, link, form control, and modal can be navigated and activated entirely via keyboard without keyboard traps.

🔊

Screen Reader Compatibility

Semantic HTML5 structures, ARIA landmark roles, and descriptive alternative text for informative instructional imagery.

🎨

High-Contrast Palette

All text meets or exceeds WCAG AA contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for normal body text and 7:1 for headers).

📝

Captions & Transcripts

Instructional video content includes closed captioning and downloadable text transcripts for auditory accessibility.

🔍

200% Text Resizing

Text can be magnified up to 200% without loss of content, clipped text, or requiring horizontal window scrolling.

📡

Low-Bandwidth Optimized

Compressed assets, audio-first options, and downloadable offline course guides designed for low-connectivity regions.

Known Limitations & Ongoing Improvements

While we strive for comprehensive accessibility, some older PDF reference archives and third-party webinar recordings may not yet feature synchronized sign-language interpretation or structured tagged tables.

Our dedicated accessibility team is actively retrofitting existing media and works with educators in our Educator Studio to ensure every newly authored course undergoes a mandatory accessibility checklist prior to publication.
