🔒 DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 2026. Compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and regional data protection regulations.

1. Overview & Commitment

Instructify Kenya (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all learners, educators, institutional leaders, and website visitors. This policy outlines how we collect, store, process, and safeguard personal information across our website (instructify.co.ke) and related LMS portals.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver educational courses, process registrations, issue certificates, and provide institutional support:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, county, school or institution name, and role.
  • CPD & Course Enrollment Data: TSC numbers (optional, for verified teacher CPD recognition), selected course programmes, and academic progress records.
  • Payment Records: M-Pesa transaction reference codes and receipt identifiers. We do not store debit/credit card numbers or M-Pesa PINs on our servers.
  • Technical Usage Data: IP address, device operating system, browser type, and timestamps used solely to ensure accessibility, account security, and error monitoring.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly under lawful bases defined by the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019:

  • To administer your enrollment in self-paced courses, workshops, and teacher CPD cohorts.
  • To generate tamper-proof digital certificates of completion verifiable on our public verification portal.
  • To provide customer support, resolve technical issues, and transmit course confirmation emails.
  • To comply with statutory reporting requirements and national educational standards.

4. Cookie Policy

We use essential cookies strictly necessary for the operation of the website, including session authentication (keeping you signed in to your learning dashboard) and accessibility preferences (high-contrast mode and text scaling). We do not sell tracking cookies or share behavioral advertising data with third parties.

5. Data Security & Retention

All communications are secured using TLS 1.3 encryption. Course records and verifiable certificates are maintained securely in accordance with educational accreditation guidelines. You retain the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer at info@instructify.co.ke.
