ACCESSIBILITY AS A PRODUCT STANDARD

Learning Designed for Different Abilities

Instructify Kenya is engineered from the ground up to support learners with visual, hearing, motor, cognitive, and learning disabilities. In full alignment with Article 54 of the Constitution of Kenya and WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines.

🔊

Screen-Reader Compatible

Semantic HTML5 landmarks, ARIA labels, and structured heading hierarchies.

⌨️

Full Keyboard Navigation

All interactive buttons, menus, tabs, and forms are fully keyboard reachable with zero traps.

💬

Captions and Transcripts

Synchronized closed captions and complete downloadable text transcripts for video/audio lessons.

🖼️

Alternative Text for Images

Meaningful descriptions for all diagrams, graphs, and educational illustrations.

🔍

Adjustable Text Size

Zoom up to 200% without loss of content, functionality, or horizontal scrolling.

🎯

Clear Focus Indicators

High-contrast, 3.5px visual focus outlines on every actionable element.

📄

Accessible Documents

Downloadable PDFs with tagged structures, machine-readable text, and bookmarks.

📝

Alternative Assessments

Flexible submission modes including text, audio responses, and untimed options.

📶

Low-Bandwidth Optimized

Lightweight asset delivery, compressed payloads, and downloadable offline lesson packs.

🤝

Accommodation Requests

A transparent, direct process to request personalized learning adjustments within 48 hours.
