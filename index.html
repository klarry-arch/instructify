CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions regarding our school workshops, venue logistics, or certification? Find quick answers below.

Where is the training conducted?

We deliver on-site workshops directly at your school campus anywhere in Kenya. Alternatively, we offer interactive virtual cohorts via Microsoft Teams or Zoom, and hybrid models blending live sessions with self-paced LMS modules.

Can the training curriculum be customized for our school?

Yes. Every institutional engagement begins with a discovery assessment where we align our training modules with your school's current device ecosystem, teacher proficiency levels, and strategic academic priorities.

What equipment and infrastructure do participating teachers need?

Participating educators only need a laptop, tablet, or smartphone with standard internet access. For on-site workshops, we provide downloadable offline templates, exercises, and supplementary instructional materials.

Do participating teachers receive verifiable certificates?

Yes. All educators who successfully complete the hands-on practical exercises receive an official Instructify Certificate of Professional Development with a verifiable credential code suitable for institutional CPD records.

How many teachers can participate in a school workshop?

We cater to departmental teams of 5 to 15 teachers as well as whole-school staff cohorts of 50 or more. For larger cohorts, we deploy co-facilitators to maintain hands-on coaching ratios.

How do we receive a formal proposal or quotation for our school board?

Simply submit the Training Request Form on this page or email info@instructify.co.ke. Our educational partnerships team will prepare a formal proposal and itemized quotation within 24 business hours.
